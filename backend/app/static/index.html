Data Provenance & Scientific Methodology

Satellite Remote Sensing Data

  • NASA-ISRO SAR (NISAR): L-band Geocoded Backscatter (GCOV Level 2) products (2025-2026). Dual-pol frequency A/B, 10m/20m pixel spacing in UTM Zone 45N.
  • Copernicus Sentinel-1A: C-band IW GRDH dual-polarization (VV+VH) observation from 28 September 2023 (pre-GLOF historical baseline).

Machine Learning Segmentation

  • Model Architecture: Gaussian Mixture Model (GMM) with Expectation-Maximization fit across multi-temporal backscatter distributions.
  • Decision Boundary: Optimal statistical thresholding separating low-backscatter specular water from rough mountain terrain, coupled with 8-connected component spatial filtering.
  • Area Calculation: Derived from georeferenced polygon geometries with WGS84 ellipsoidal transformation.

Geospatial Supporting Datasets

  • Copernicus 30m DEM: Slope, elevation, and terrain shadow masking.
  • OpenStreetMap Teesta Basin: 18,760 mapped infrastructure assets (bridges, settlements, roads).
  • Randolph Glacier Inventory (RGI v7): South Asia East glacier boundaries and hypsometry.
